Custom Invoice Reports

Professional invoice customization module for Odoo 15. Create fully customizable invoice reports with advanced formatting options, logo management, font controls, and multi-format support for both Accounting and Point of Sale modules.

Key Features

Custom Logo Management

Upload your custom logo, adjust size (width & height), and align it left, center, or right. Logo alignment automatically adjusts company name and address positioning.

Complete Font Customization

Control font sizes and bold settings for all invoice elements: headers, table columns, totals, amounts, company details, and customer information.

Multiple Paper Formats

Support for A4, A5, Legal, and Letter paper sizes. Choose the format that best fits your business needs.

Previous Credit Display

Automatically calculate and display previous credit amounts on invoices, helping you track customer balances effectively.

POS Integration

Full support for Point of Sale invoices with dedicated custom report. Print custom invoices directly from POS with a dedicated button.

Table Customization

Customize font sizes and bold settings for Description, Quantity, Unit Price, Taxes, and Amount columns independently.

How to Use

01

Go to Accounting → Configuration → Invoice Report Settings to configure your invoice report preferences. Create separate settings for Accounting and POS reports.

02

In the Logo Settings tab, upload your Custom Logo, set Logo Alignment (Left/Center/Right), adjust Logo Width and Logo Height, and select Paper Size (A4, A5, Legal, Letter).

03

Configure Company Header Settings with separate font sizes and bold options for Company Name Label, Company Name Value, Company Address Label, and Company Address Value.

04

In the Header Font Sizes tab, customize font sizes and bold settings for Invoice Number, Customer Name, Invoice Date (Heading & Value), Due Date (Heading & Value), Source (Heading & Value), and Reference.

05

In the Table Font Sizes tab, customize font sizes and bold settings for table columns: Description, Quantity, Unit Price, Taxes, and Amount independently.

06

Configure Previous Credit Font Sizes and Previous Invoices Font Sizes with separate heading and value font sizes and bold options.

07

Customize Total & Amount Due Font Sizes with separate font sizes and bold options for Total Heading, Total Value, Amount Due Heading, and Amount Due Value.

08

In Accounting, open any invoice and click the Download PDF button to generate the customized invoice report with all your settings applied.

09

View your customized invoice report with custom logo, adjusted font sizes, previous credit display, and all formatting applied according to your settings.

10

In Point of Sale, use the Custom Invoice (POS) button next to the Validate button to print custom invoices directly from POS with your configured settings.
